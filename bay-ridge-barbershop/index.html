The experience

Old-school shop, neighborhood feel

Step into a welcoming, clean and cozy shop with antique barber chairs, classic decor and friendly banter. Bay Ridge Barbershop has been family-oriented since 2012 and competes on quality, not price.

We're known for precise straight-razor, hot-towel shaves and traditional cuts, for everyday trims and for the big days too: weddings, birthdays and anniversaries.
